# Evaluation mapping

| Evaluation question                                                                           | Prototype support                                                                                                                                                                         | Evidence collected outside the prototype                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| EQ 1.1 — Can shoppers locate and interpret appearance, quality, price and savings?            | Three standardised product scenarios; Condition B product cards and details separate the four information components; shareable session URLs keep the screen stable.                      | Correct/partial/incorrect coding, task time, hesitation, repeated reading, help requests and Microsoft Forms ratings. |
| EQ 1.2 — Are product details, prices and savings correct?                                     | One JSON record per product; integer-cent prices; savings and percentage derived in code; automated consistency check; unconfirmed records visibly marked `not verified` for researchers. | Comparison with the approved source sheet and a 3–5 participant accuracy check.                                       |
| EQ 2.1 — When and why would shoppers check the label/details?                                 | Shopping-list context, product cards, optional detail opening and realistic product-choice actions support journey discussion.                                                            | Journey questionnaire and five semi-structured interviews; do not treat prototype clicks as real in-store behaviour.  |
| EQ 2.2 — Does clear information and savings framing affect willingness, confidence and value? | A/B comparison mode holds product, image, quantity and current price constant; A shows basic information and B adds appearance, quality and explicit savings.                             | Within-participant choices, three 7-point ratings, follow-up questions and observation notes.                         |

## Recommended session order

1. Open Research setup and select the required evaluation flow.
2. Select the product scenario and condition before the participant sees the screen.
3. Copy the stable session URL and record the prototype version.
4. Hand the screen to the participant without explaining label content.
5. Record observations and answers on the approved research sheet.
6. Mark the task complete, then open the team’s Microsoft Forms questionnaire.

## Scope limitation

The prototype supports a controlled formative evaluation. It does not prove real in-store attention, actual purchasing behaviour, long-term sales effects, food-waste reduction or population-level findings.
